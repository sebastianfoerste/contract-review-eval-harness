## U-1 Health surveillance records

Operations has decided that the platform will also hold occupational health
surveillance records for around 180 laboratory employees who work with hazardous
substances. These records will be uploaded from the first day of use. This
replaces the statement in our memo of 22 September 2026 that no special
categories of personal data will be uploaded.

## U-2 Earlier go-live

The management board has moved go-live forward to 15 January 2027. The business
wants to sign by the end of November 2026 and asks which points can be closed
now.

## U-3 Audit of data centres

Internal Audit has confirmed that the exclusion of data centres from on-site
audits is acceptable if Cverbio supplies the hosting provider's current ISO/IEC
27001 certificate and SOC 2 report every year.

## U-4 Works council

The works council has not yet been informed that employee data can be accessed
from outside the EEA.

## U-5 What we still do not have

Annex 3 was not attached to the Cverbio response. We have also not received
Cverbio's intra-group agreement or any transfer impact assessment.
