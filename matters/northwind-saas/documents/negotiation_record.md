# Negotiation record (SYNTHETIC)

Kept by: Legal, Northwind Materials GmbH
Matter: Cverbio LabTrace platform

This record is fabricated for evaluation purposes. No real company, person or
transaction is described.

## Entry 1. Kick-off call, 14 September 2026

Attendees: Northwind procurement lead and Head of Legal; Cverbio account
executive and solutions engineer.

The Cverbio account executive stated that customer data is never used to train
Cverbio models.

The solutions engineer stated that all production data is hosted in Frankfurt.

On sub-processors, Cverbio said it uses a handful of them and would send the
list after the call. The list has not arrived.

Northwind asked for on-site audit rights. Cverbio answered that it provides its
SOC 2 report to every customer and grants on-site audits to none.

The account executive stated that Cverbio's standard paper is not negotiable for
contracts below USD 500,000 per year, with the exception of the Data Processing
Agreement.

Both sides agreed in principle on 99.5% uptime and on an initial term of twelve
months. Liability, the model-training licence, audits and fees remain open.

## Entry 2. Email from Cverbio legal counsel, 17 September 2026

Cverbio's counsel wrote that, where the Software-as-a-Service Agreement and the
Data Processing Agreement conflict, the Software-as-a-Service Agreement prevails.
Neither draft contains an order of precedence.

## Entry 3. Email from Cverbio support lead, 18 September 2026

In reply to a question about the pilot, the support lead wrote that Cverbio's
support and model operations teams are located in the United States and India
and can access production data for troubleshooting.

## Entry 4. Internal note, 19 September 2026

contained customer contact data and was uploaded under the non-disclosure
