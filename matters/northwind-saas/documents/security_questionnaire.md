# Vendor security questionnaire, completed (SYNTHETIC)

Questionnaire issued by: Northwind Materials GmbH
Completed by: Cverbio Labs Inc.
Date completed: 8 September 2026

This questionnaire is fabricated for evaluation purposes. No real company,
person or product is described.

## SQ-01 Certifications

Answer: An ISO/IEC 27001 certificate is held by Cverbio Analytics Inc. A SOC 2
Type II report is available under a non-disclosure agreement.

## SQ-02 Hosting location

Answer: All Customer Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt region).

## SQ-03 Access from outside the EEA

Answer: Support engineers may access production systems remotely on a
follow-the-sun basis.

## SQ-04 Encryption

Answer: Customer Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and with TLS 1.2 or
higher in transit.

## SQ-05 Incident notification

Answer: Customers are notified of security incidents affecting their data within
24 hours of confirmation.

## SQ-06 Sub-processors

Answer: Four sub-processors are currently used. The list is provided on request.

## SQ-07 Use of customer data for service improvement

Answer: Customer Data is used to improve the service only in aggregated and
de-identified form.

## SQ-08 Deletion after termination

Answer: Customer Data is deleted from production systems within 30 days of
termination and from backups within 90 days.

## SQ-09 Penetration testing

Answer: An independent penetration test is performed once a year. A summary of
the results is available on request.

## SQ-10 Business continuity

Answer: The recovery point objective is 24 hours and the recovery time objective
is 72 hours.

## SQ-11 Customer audits

Answer: Customers may review the SOC 2 report once a year. On-site audits are
not offered.
