# Instruction memo to outside counsel (SYNTHETIC)

From: Head of Legal, Northwind Materials GmbH
To: outside counsel
Date: 22 September 2026
Subject: Cverbio LabTrace platform, review of vendor paper

This memo is fabricated for evaluation purposes. No real company, person or
transaction is described.

## 1. What we are buying

Northwind is replacing its laboratory information system with LabTrace, a hosted
analytics platform supplied by Cverbio. The platform will hold the test results of
our four materials laboratories in Germany and will be business-critical from the
first day of use. Go-live is planned for 1 March 2027. The quoted fees are EUR
240,000 per year.

Cverbio sent three documents on its own paper: a mutual non-disclosure agreement,
a Software-as-a-Service Agreement and a Data Processing Agreement. The
non-disclosure agreement was signed by our procurement team on 2 September 2026,
before Legal was involved. The other two documents are unsigned drafts.

## 2. Data that will be on the platform

The platform will hold laboratory test results, contact data for around 4,000
business customers and user accounts for around 600 employees. No special
categories of personal data will be uploaded.

## 3. What matters to us, in this order

1. Our data must not be used for model training or for any other purpose of the
   vendor. This is not negotiable for personal data.
2. Our data must be stored and accessed only within the European Economic Area.
   The works council has been told that employee data stays in the EEA.
3. We must be able to leave. We need a complete export in a standard format and
   continued access for at least 90 days after the contract ends.
4. We need price certainty for the initial term.
5. On liability, the management board has approved a general cap of not less than
   200% of the annual fees, provided that breaches of data protection and
   confidentiality obligations sit outside that cap.

## 4. What we can live with

The business accepts an uptime figure of 99.5% and an initial term of twelve
months. Automatic renewal is acceptable only if we can stop it on short notice.

## 5. Constraints you should know about

Internal Audit requires on-site audit rights for every critical vendor. Our
automotive customers audit us and require us to pass that right down our supply
chain.

A pilot ran in August 2026. Procurement uploaded a pilot data set to LabTrace
before any Data Processing Agreement was in place.

## 6. What we do not have

We have not received the order form, the list of sub-processors or the
description of technical and organisational measures. Procurement believes that
our contracting party is Cverbio Labs Inc., but the pilot invoices came from
Cverbio Analytics Inc. We do not know which company operates the platform.

## 7. Who can decide what

The Head of Legal can approve any position that the contracting playbook marks
as a fallback. Anything beyond a fallback needs the joint approval of the
General Counsel and the Chief Financial Officer. Any arrangement under which
employee data can be reached from outside the EEA must be notified to the works
council before signature.

## 8. What we need from you

Tell us where the three documents contradict each other or contradict what
Cverbio has told us, what position we should take on each term, and what you
cannot answer without more information. We want to sign by the end of November
2026.
