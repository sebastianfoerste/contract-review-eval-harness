# Cverbio response to the Northwind mark-up (SYNTHETIC)

From: legal counsel, Cverbio
To: Head of Legal, Northwind Materials GmbH
Date: 9 October 2026

This response is fabricated for evaluation purposes. No real company, person or
transaction is described.

Cverbio has reviewed the Northwind mark-up of 29 September 2026. Its positions
are set out below. All terms not mentioned in this response remain as drafted.

## R-1 Contracting entities

The Software-as-a-Service Agreement will be signed by Cverbio Labs Inc. The Data
Processing Agreement will remain with Cverbio Analytics Inc., which operates the
platform.

## R-2 Service improvement licence

Section 8 of the Software-as-a-Service Agreement will be limited to Customer
Data that has been aggregated and de-identified, and Customer Personal Data is
excluded from it. The licence remains perpetual and irrevocable and continues to
cover derivative products.

## R-3 Sub-processors

Cverbio will give 30 days' prior notice of a new sub-processor by email. The
Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the objection is
not resolved, the Customer's sole remedy is to terminate the affected service
without refund of prepaid fees. The current list of sub-processors is attached
as Annex 3.

## R-4 Personal data breaches

Cverbio will notify the Controller of a personal data breach within 72 hours of
becoming aware of it.

## R-5 Liability

The general cap is raised to 200% of the annual fees. A separate cap of 300% of
the annual fees applies to breaches of data protection and confidentiality
obligations.

## R-6 Audit

The Customer may carry out one on-site audit per year at Cverbio's offices on 60
days' notice and at its own cost. Data centres are excluded from on-site audits.

## R-7 Fees

Fees are fixed for the initial term. After the initial term, increases are
limited to 7% per year.

## R-8 Data location

Cverbio confirms that Customer Data is stored in Frankfurt. Remote access by
support staff in the United States and India is required for 24/7 support and is
covered by Cverbio's intra-group agreement.

## R-9 Exit

The Customer may export its data for 30 days after termination. Cverbio will
delete Customer Data from production systems within 30 days after that period
and from backups within 90 days.

## R-10 Timing

Cverbio will hold the quoted price if both documents are signed by 30 November
2026.
