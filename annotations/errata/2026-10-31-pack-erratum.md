## 1. Art. 28(3)(f) GDPR: Art. 34 and Art. 36 were missing

As sent:

    - Art. 28(3)(f), Art. 33 and Art. 35: breach notification and impact assessments.

Corrected:

    - Art. 28(3)(f), Art. 33, Art. 34, Art. 35 and Art. 36: assistance with the obligations in Art. 32 to 36 (breach notification to the supervisory authority and communication to the data subject, impact assessments and prior consultation).

Art. 28(3)(f) reads: the processor "assists the controller in ensuring compliance with
the obligations pursuant to Articles 32 to 36 taking into account the nature of
processing and the information available to the processor". Art. 34 (communication of a
personal data breach to the data subject) and Art. 36 (prior consultation) are inside
that range and were not listed.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, OJ L 119, 4.5.2016, Art. 28(3)(f) at p. 49, Art. 34 at
p. 52, Art. 36 at p. 54. ELI http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj; Official Journal
text read at
https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/3e485e15-11bd-11e6-ba9a-01aa75ed71a1.0006.01/DOC_1
on 2026-10-31.

## 2. Chapter V GDPR runs to Art. 50, not Art. 49

As sent:

    - Chapter V, Art. 44 to 49: transfers to third countries.

Corrected:

    - Chapter V, Art. 44 to 50: transfers to third countries.

Chapter V ("Transfers of personal data to third countries or international
organisations") comprises Articles 44 to 50; Art. 50 (international cooperation for the
protection of personal data) is its last article, and Chapter VI begins at Art. 51.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2016/679, OJ L 119, 4.5.2016, pp. 60 to 65. ELI
http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj; Official Journal text read at
https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/3e485e15-11bd-11e6-ba9a-01aa75ed71a1.0006.01/DOC_1
on 2026-10-31.

## 3. EDPB Opinion 22/2024 was not listed under Guidance

Added after the Guidelines 07/2020 entry:

    - EDPB Opinion 22/2024 on certain obligations following from the reliance on processor(s) and sub-processor(s), adopted 7 October 2024.

The opinion was adopted on 7 October 2024 at the request of the Danish supervisory
authority. It bears on sub-processor authorisation (para. 27: under a general
authorisation the processor should give the controller the opportunity to approve a
list of sub-processors when the authorisation is signed and the opportunity, including
a sufficient timeframe, to object to subsequent changes), on the availability of the
identity of all processors and sub-processors (paras 31 to 32) and on the verification
of their guarantees (paras 47 to 49). It is guidance, not law: a risk that rests on it
takes the `source_category` `regulatory_guidance`.

Source:
https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2024-10/edpb_opinion_202422_relianceonprocessors-sub-processors_en.pdf,
read on 2026-10-31.
