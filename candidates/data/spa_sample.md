# Share Purchase Agreement, Excerpt (SYNTHETIC)

This excerpt of a Share Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Marlow Industrial
Holding GmbH ("Seller") and Tessarine Capital Partners IV SCSp ("Purchaser") concerning all
shares in Kestrel Sensorik GmbH ("Company"). This document is fabricated for evaluation
purposes and is not a real agreement. The review perspective is the Purchaser's.

## 1. Sale and Transfer

The Seller sells and transfers all shares in the Company to the Purchaser with economic
effect as of 1 January (the "Effective Date"). The transfer is subject to payment of the
Purchase Price.

## 2. Purchase Price and Locked Box

The Purchase Price is a fixed amount of EUR 48,000,000. The Seller confirms that no Leakage
has occurred since the Effective Date. Payments to the Seller's affiliates under existing
management service agreements, in whatever amount, are Permitted Leakage.

## 3. Closing Conditions

Closing is subject to merger control clearance. The Purchaser bears the risk of any
condition or remedy imposed by a competition authority and shall accept any such remedy.

## 4. Seller's Guarantees

The Seller guarantees by way of an independent guarantee within the meaning of section 311
(1) of the German Civil Code (BGB) that the statements in this clause are correct as of the
signing date:

- The Seller holds unencumbered title to the shares.
- The annual accounts have been prepared in accordance with applicable accounting principles.
- To the Seller's best knowledge, the Company is not party to any litigation exceeding EUR 5,000,000.
- To the Seller's best knowledge, the Company's material agreements contain no change of control rights.

Seller's knowledge means the actual knowledge of the Seller's managing director on the
signing date, without any duty of enquiry.

## 5. Disclosure

All facts contained in the virtual data room as of the signing date are deemed disclosed
and known to the Purchaser, whether or not they were fairly disclosed. Claims are excluded
to the extent a matter is deemed disclosed.

## 6. Limitation of Liability

The Seller's aggregate liability under this Agreement, including for title guarantees, is
capped at five per cent of the Purchase Price. Individual claims below EUR 500,000 are
excluded, and claims may only be brought once their aggregate exceeds EUR 2,500,000, in
which case only the excess is recoverable. All claims become time-barred six months after
Closing.

## 7. Tax Indemnity

The Seller indemnifies the Purchaser against taxes of the Company for periods before the
Effective Date, subject to the limitations in clause 6.

## 8. Conduct Between Signing and Closing

The Seller shall procure that the Company conducts its business in the ordinary course. No
consent of the Purchaser is required for any measure the Seller considers commercially
reasonable.

## 9. Non-Compete

The Purchaser shall not, for five years after Closing, compete with any business of the
Seller's group anywhere in the world.

## 10. Assignment

The Purchaser may not assign rights under this Agreement, including to financing banks,
without the Seller's prior written consent.

## 11. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by German law. Disputes are finally settled by the ordinary
courts at the Seller's registered seat.
