# Data Processing Agreement (SYNTHETIC)

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between Northwind
Materials GmbH ("Controller") and Cverbio Analytics Inc. ("Processor") pursuant
to Article 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. This document is fabricated for
evaluation purposes and is not a real agreement.

## 1. Subject Matter and Instructions

The Processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from
the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third
country, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which the
Processor is subject.

## 2. Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that persons authorised to process the personal data
have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate
statutory obligation of confidentiality.

## 3. Security of Processing

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures
to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, having regard to the
state of the art and the costs of implementation. A summary of such measures is
available on request.

## 4. Sub-processors

The Processor may engage sub-processors at its own discretion and shall inform
the Controller of any intended changes within a reasonable period after such
changes take effect. The Controller waives any right to object to the
appointment of a sub-processor.

## 5. Assistance with Data Subject Rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Processor shall assist the
Controller by appropriate technical and organisational measures for the
fulfilment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests for exercising
the data subject's rights laid down in Chapter III.

## 6. Personal Data Breaches and Impact Assessments

The Processor shall notify the Controller of a personal data breach within
fourteen (14) days of becoming aware of it, and shall provide reasonable
assistance with data protection impact assessments where commercially agreed.

## 7. Deletion and Return

Following termination of the services, the Processor may retain personal data
for its own legitimate business purposes, including product improvement and
model training, for an indefinite period.

## 8. Audits and Inspections

The Processor shall make available to the Controller a third-party audit
certificate once per calendar year. On-site inspections and audits by the
Controller or an auditor mandated by the Controller are expressly excluded.

## 9. International Transfers

The Processor may transfer personal data to its affiliates in third countries as
necessary for the provision of the services. The parties shall address any
required transfer mechanism separately if and when the need arises.

## 10. Governing Law

This DPA is governed by the laws of Germany.
